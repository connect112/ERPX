Data Deletion

Last updated 13 October 2026

You can ask GIR Technologies (Pentrix) to delete the personal information we hold about you, including anything from your Instagram comments and messages to @pentrix16.

How to ask

  • Email info@girtechnologies.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  • Tell us your Instagram username (and your name and email if you also gave them to us), so we can find your records.
  • We may ask you to confirm you are the owner of the account, for example by messaging us from it.

What we delete

  • Your comments and your direct-message conversations with us, and the text of our replies to them.
  • Your Instagram username in our records.
  • Your lead or admissions record, on request, unless we must keep part of it to meet a legal or accounting obligation. We will tell you if that applies.

How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request and tell you by email when it is done.

Removing app access on Instagram

You can also remove any app's access to your account yourself: in Instagram go to Settings, then Apps and websites, and remove it.

Questions

Write to info@girtechnologies.com. Our full Privacy Policy is at https://erp.pentrix.in/privacy.
